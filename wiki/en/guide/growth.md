# Growth

Strength, fitness, the three firearm skills, cold tolerance and heat tolerance all grow by doing things, up to level 10 each. Repeating the same thing gives less and less experience (no farming), but there is no daily cap. A corner notice shows each level up

Open the growth page with K, or with the Growth button on the character page

## Firearms

Pistols, long guns and shotguns are trained separately: hits, kills and reloads give experience. At levels 0 / 4 / 7 the reload tier is novice, experienced and practised; only the practised tier can reload while aiming

## Cold and heat tolerance

Every minute spent cold or hot gives experience. Each level moves the comfortable temperature range a little further that way (values in °C). The "cold hardy / heat hardy" background or trait starts the skill at level 3; "cold sensitive / heat sensitive" moves the comfortable range the other way
