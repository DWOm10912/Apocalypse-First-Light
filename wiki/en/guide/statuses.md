# Status Effects

A column of round status icons sits above the survival module in the lower left, shown only while a status applies: green is a buff, brown is a debuff, blue is cold or wet, red is dangerous. The most urgent sits nearest the module. When you gain a status (or a worse stage of it), its rim flashes and its name and effect slide out beside it for 4 seconds. Timed statuses (a stomach bug, fuel on your body) show the time left as an arc and a number
