## Getting one

<StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-21" /> <StatusTag s="creative" /> The Creative inventory's Vehicles tab has <ItemIcon id="pickup_t15" />. Use it on the ground to open the spawner:

- On the left, a 3D preview of the truck; drag to rotate it
- Paint: 8 factory colours, including forest green with a beige band
- Wear: a 0–100% slider, or New, Used or Worn
- Fuel: from empty to a full <V j="PickupTruckEntity.TANK" u="L" /> tank
- Key location: the driver's sun visor (default), the glovebox, in the ignition, or random in the cab
- Press Place and the truck appears where you clicked, facing the way you look. Its key already belongs to it. In Creative the item is not used up

A dev command can also spawn one (operator permission):

```
/afl dev vehicle spawn [colour]
```

It doesn't appear in the world on its own

<StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-21" /> The grille carries Halstead's chrome pine badge in the middle; HALSTEAD is pressed into the tailgate

## Driving

- Sit in the driver's seat and **hold G** to start it, see [Key](#key)
- <StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-21" /> In the driver's seat your hands rest in your lap, so you can hold and use things. Press **Use** on the steering wheel to take hold of it: only then do the pedals, steering and gears work. Press **Use** again to let go. Letting go at a crawl pulls the handbrake; letting go on the move, the truck coasts
- **R / F** shift up and down; the clutch is automatic
- **X** switches the transfer case; 4H / 4L need you below 20 km/h
- You can get out below <V j="PickupTruckEntity.GET_OUT_SPEED" u="m/s" />
- <StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-21" /> From a front seat, open your own door first: press **Use** on it, then **Sneak** to get out. With the door shut, Sneak only tells you to open the door. The door stays open after you get out
- <StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-21" /> Get out with the engine running and it keeps idling with the key in the ignition, until it runs dry or someone switches it off
- Holding the wheel, both hands are on the wheel and the gear lever: a weapon in hand is put away, you cannot switch to one and you cannot use anything else. Passengers are not affected
- **Caps Lock** hides the key hints at the top right and shows them again; your choice is remembered
- <StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-19" /> **F5** switches to the orbit camera: it chases behind the truck, the mouse swings it round, scroll for distance
- All keys: [Controls](../guide/controls)

## Key

<StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-21" /> Every T15 has one key of its own <ItemIcon id="pickup_t15_key" />, and it starts only that truck. Without the key it will not start

- <StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-21" /> Every vehicle model has its own key, with the maker's badge set into the head. The T15 key carries Halstead's chrome three-bar pine badge on both faces, the same one as on the grille
- The ignition is in the dash to the right of the wheel; you see it through the gap on the wheel's right side
- <StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-21" /> **Hold G** in the driver's seat: the camera turns to the keyhole, your right hand takes the key from your gear and puts it in, turns it clockwise to ON, waits about 0.8 s for the glow plugs, then turns it to START until the engine catches, and the camera comes back. The key doesn't have to be in your hand, only on you (hand, gear slots, backpack or inventory)
- With the key already in, **holding G** just turns it; with the engine running, **holding G** switches it off
- Let go of **G** partway and it stops: the key springs back but stays in the ignition. With no key in the lock or on you, you get "No key"
- You can also press **Use** on the keyhole: with the key on you, your right hand puts it in; with it in and the engine off, your right hand pulls it out and keeps it
- Leaving the driver's seat with the engine off, you pull the key and take it with you, then get out; if you have no room for it, it stays in the ignition. With the engine running, the key stays in
- With the engine off you can also take the key: sit in front and press Use on the keyhole
- A truck you find usually has no key in it. A few still have it in the ignition, or left in the cab: behind a sun visor, in the glovebox, in the ashtray, on a seat
- From the driver's or passenger's seat, press Use on a visor, the glovebox or the ashtray to open it; if the key is inside, press again to take it
- For testing: `/afl dev vehicle key <spot|random>` puts the key at a spot or a random one, `/afl dev vehicle key give` hands it to you
- <StatusTag s="planned" /> Without the key: take the lower column shroud off with a screwdriver and hotwire it

## Tyres

<StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-19" /> On dirt, gravel, sand, grass and snow the tyres throw chips and dust behind them, more when they spin or skid; in rain they throw streaks of water and mist off wet asphalt, more the faster you go (with shaders the pack draws it; Aureveil does not yet); on dry hard ground only a hard skid smokes. None with Particles set to Minimal

## Lights

- **H** toggles the headlights; hold for high beam. Brake lights come on by themselves
- **← / →** turn signals, **↓** hazards, blinking about 90 times a minute
- Without shaders the headlights really light the road and walls (needs Embeddium), for up to 3 trucks within 128 blocks
- With a shader pack the pack handles lighting

## Wipers

**C** cycles: off → intermittent (<V j="WiperSystem.PAUSE" u="s" /> pause) → low (<V j="WiperSystem.LOW_STROKE" digits="2" u="s" /> per stroke) → high (<V j="WiperSystem.HIGH_STROKE" digits="2" u="s" /> per stroke)

In rain, drops gather on the windshield, rear window and door windows; the wipers clear what they sweep

- <StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-19" /> You hear each wiper sweep; on asphalt the tyres hiss on the wet road; in the cab you hear the rain drumming on the roof and the glass
- <StatusTag s="planned" /> Washer fluid

## Heater and A/C

<StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-19" /> **V** cycles: off → heat → A/C. The sliders on the heater panel move with it, and the dash strip lights a heat or a snowflake lamp

- In the driver's and passenger's seats you stay out of the rain, and the cab has its own air: with the doors shut a little warmer than outside, hot when it stands in the sun; with a door open the same as outside
- **Heat** comes from the engine's coolant and burns no extra fuel: nothing while the engine is cold; once the coolant warms up (from about <V j="CabClimate.WARM_FROM" u="°C" />, full at <V j="CabClimate.WARM_FULL" u="°C" />) the cab warms to about <V j="CabClimate.HEAT_SET" u="°C" /> within a few minutes. The key must be on
- **A/C** runs a compressor off the engine: only with the engine running, and it burns a little more fuel; it cools to about <V j="CabClimate.COOL_SET" u="°C" /> at best
- With a door open the heater and the A/C do far less
- The cargo bed is not the cab: you still get rained on there

## Exhaust

<StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-19" /> The tail pipe (behind the right rear wheel, pointing back and out) is open, sooted inside, and smokes the way an old turbo diesel does. Other players' trucks smoke too

- **Starting**: a puff of dark grey as the engine catches
- **Cold engine**: whitish grey and thick, thinning as the coolant warms (gone by about <V j="TruckExhaust.WARM_AT" u="°C" />)
- **Warm and idling**: almost nothing; a little more under load
- **Flooring it at low revs**: a short dark puff until the turbo comes in (below about <V j="TruckExhaust.LUG_RPM" u="rpm" />)
- **Cold air**: white vapour all the time below about <V j="TruckExhaust.VAPOUR_FROM" u="°C" />, more the colder it is
- The smoke stays where it left the pipe, so it trails behind a moving truck
