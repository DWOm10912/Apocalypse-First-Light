## Getting one

For now it can only be spawned with a dev command (operator permission):

```
/afl dev vehicle spawn [colour]
```

There is no item for it yet, and it doesn't appear in the world on its own

## Driving

- Sit in the driver's seat and **hold G** to crank; let go once the engine catches
- **R / F** shift up and down; the clutch is automatic
- **X** switches the transfer case; 4H / 4L need you below 20 km/h
- You can get out below <V j="PickupTruckEntity.GET_OUT_SPEED" u="m/s" />
- While driving, both hands are on the wheel and the gear lever: a weapon in hand is put away when you take the driver's seat, and you cannot switch to one until you leave it. Passengers are not affected
- **Caps Lock** hides the key hints at the top right and shows them again; your choice is remembered
- <StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-19" /> **F5** switches to the orbit camera: it chases behind the truck, the mouse swings it round, scroll for distance
- All keys: [Controls](../guide/controls)

## Tyres

<StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-19" /> On dirt, gravel, sand, grass and snow the tyres throw chips and dust behind them, more when they spin or skid; in rain they throw streaks of water and mist off wet asphalt, more the faster you go (with shaders the pack draws it; Aureveil does not yet); on dry hard ground only a hard skid smokes. None with Particles set to Minimal

## Lights

- **H** toggles the headlights; hold for high beam. Brake lights come on by themselves
- **← / →** turn signals, **↓** hazards, blinking about 90 times a minute
- Without shaders the headlights really light the road and walls (needs Embeddium), for up to 3 trucks within 128 blocks
- With a shader pack the pack handles lighting

## Wipers

**C** cycles: off → intermittent (<V j="WiperSystem.PAUSE" u="s" /> pause) → low (<V j="WiperSystem.LOW_STROKE" digits="2" u="s" /> per stroke) → high (<V j="WiperSystem.HIGH_STROKE" digits="2" u="s" /> per stroke)

In rain, drops gather on the windshield, rear window and door windows; the wipers clear what they sweep

- <StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-19" /> You hear each wiper sweep; on asphalt the tyres hiss on the wet road; in the cab you hear the rain drumming on the roof and the glass
- <StatusTag s="planned" /> Washer fluid

## Heater and A/C

<StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-19" /> **V** cycles: off → heat → A/C. The sliders on the heater panel move with it, and the dash strip lights a heat or a snowflake lamp

- In the driver's and passenger's seats you stay out of the rain, and the cab has its own air: with the doors shut a little warmer than outside, hot when it stands in the sun; with a door open the same as outside
- **Heat** comes from the engine's coolant and burns no extra fuel: nothing while the engine is cold; once the coolant warms up (from about <V j="CabClimate.WARM_FROM" u="°C" />, full at <V j="CabClimate.WARM_FULL" u="°C" />) the cab warms to about <V j="CabClimate.HEAT_SET" u="°C" /> within a few minutes. The key must be on
- **A/C** runs a compressor off the engine: only with the engine running, and it burns a little more fuel; it cools to about <V j="CabClimate.COOL_SET" u="°C" /> at best
- With a door open the heater and the A/C do far less
- The cargo bed is not the cab: you still get rained on there

## Exhaust

<StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-19" /> The tail pipe (behind the right rear wheel, pointing back and out) is open, sooted inside, and smokes the way an old turbo diesel does. Other players' trucks smoke too

- **Starting**: a puff of dark grey as the engine catches
- **Cold engine**: whitish grey and thick, thinning as the coolant warms (gone by about <V j="TruckExhaust.WARM_AT" u="°C" />)
- **Warm and idling**: almost nothing; a little more under load
- **Flooring it at low revs**: a short dark puff until the turbo comes in (below about <V j="TruckExhaust.LUG_RPM" u="rpm" />)
- **Cold air**: white vapour all the time below about <V j="TruckExhaust.VAPOUR_FROM" u="°C" />, more the colder it is
- The smoke stays where it left the pipe, so it trails behind a moving truck
