# Vehicles

AFL vehicles are machines you actually operate: start the engine, shift gears, refuel, and use the lights, wipers and doors. There is one vehicle so far

| Vehicle | Type | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Halstead Tamarack T15](./t15) | Diesel pickup, 6 seats | <StatusTag s="test" /> |

<Shot k="hero_vehicle" zh="T15 概念图（游戏内截图待补）" en="T15 concept sheet (in-game screenshot coming)" />

## What you can do now

- Spawn a T15 with a dev command and drive it on highways and dirt roads
- Refuel with diesel, use the lights and wipers, honk, signal
- Open the doors, hood, tailgate and fuel filler
