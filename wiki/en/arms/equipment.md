# Equipment

You have five equipment slots: back, rig, wrist, ears and holster

## Carrying

<StatusTag s="creative" />

| Item | Slot | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Field Chest Rig | Rig | 4 magazine pouches: three 1×2, one 2×2 |
| Canvas Backpack | Back | 5×6 cells |
| Hiking Backpack | Back | 6×10 cells |
| Pistol Holster | Holster | Lets a pistol use slot 2 |
| Gun Sling | Fitted to a long gun | Lets the long gun use slot 1 |

See [Inventory and Weight](../guide/inventory)

## Tools

| Item | Effect | How to get |
|---|---|---|
| Simple Hearing Protection | Worn on the ears; eases ringing from gunfire and muffles outside sound | Crafted: iron ingot, leather, wool |
| Wrist Thermometer | Worn on the wrist; always shows air temperature | Crafted: leather, glass pane, copper ingot |
| Clinical Thermometer | Hold right click for about 3 s to take your temperature | Crafted: glass panes, red dye |
| Geiger Counter | Shows the current dose rate | <StatusTag s="creative" /> |
| Crowbar | Melee weapon; also removes tunnel fixtures | <StatusTag s="creative" /> |
