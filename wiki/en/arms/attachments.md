## Changing attachments

- **In the field**: hold the gun and press **Z**; it takes about 2.5 s
- **Gun Maintenance Bench**: lay the gun on the bench and swap parts in a top-down view of the whole gun. The bench doesn't repair guns and has no recipes
- The C.A.T can't change attachments

## Gun sling

<StatusTag s="integrated" />

- A long gun needs a gun sling to be carried on the back, in slot 1
- The sling goes on the rear swivel under the stock and is changed like any other attachment: press **Z** or use a gun maintenance bench, click the swivel and pick a sling you carry from the row of candidates. A sling you take off goes back into your inventory, or drops at your feet if there is no room
- Clicking a gun with a sling in the inventory no longer fits it. Slings fitted before still count; the first time one is taken off or replaced at the swivel it becomes an attachment
- For now the BR-51, HR-55 and Silverwood 12 take a sling. The C.A.T does not
- A fitted sling hangs from the front and rear swivels where a real one would: on the BR-51 under the front of the handguard and under the stock, on the HR-55 under the handguard and under the stock, on the Silverwood 12 under the front of the barrel rib and under the stock. A steel snap hook clips each end on
- The strap has real physics: it sags and sways when you turn the gun in the inspection view, walk or aim, like webbing with some weight to it, settles quickly, and does not pass through the gun, its magazine or your arms
- Held in the hands (first and third person alike) the strap is off the shoulder and hangs between the two swivels; carried on the back it runs over the left shoulder and across the body
- On a maintenance bench or on the ground the strap lies flat beside the gun in a smooth curve; on a gun rack it hangs down
