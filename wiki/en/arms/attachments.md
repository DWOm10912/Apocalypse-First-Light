## Changing attachments

- **In the field**: hold the gun and press **Z**; it takes about 2.5 s
- **Gun Maintenance Bench**: lay the gun on the bench and swap parts in a top-down view of the whole gun. The bench doesn't repair guns and has no recipes
- The C.A.T can't change attachments

## Suppressors
