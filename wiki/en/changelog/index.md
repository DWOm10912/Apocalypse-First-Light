## 1.0.0-alpha (in development)

AFL hasn't been publicly released. What you can already play under this version:

- **World**: the Federal Six States map, 20 biomes, AFL's own trees and felling, interstates and state highways, tunnels
- **Survival**: stamina, thirst, temperature, weight, radiation, the grid inventory, permadeath and succession
- **Guns**: pistols, rifles, shotguns and more (see [Guns](../arms/)), with reloads, inspection, fire modes, attachments and the maintenance bench
- **Vehicles**: the T15 pickup, 5-speed manual, 4×4, lights, wipers
- **Weather**: shader-free rain, dynamic headlights
- **Industry**: generators, distribution and hidden wiring, processing machines, fuel station equipment
- **Infected**: sight, hearing, breaking in

Content tagged <StatusTag s="creative" /> or <StatusTag s="test" /> isn't available in normal survival yet
