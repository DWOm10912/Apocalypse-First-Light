# World and Exploration

New worlds default to the **AFL Federal Six States**: an island of about 20×16 km split into six states, with mountains, lakes, coasts, plateaus and canyons, linked by highways

## In the world now

- Terrain, rivers, lakes, coasts and 20 biomes
- Six AFL tree species in place of vanilla trees
- Interstates and state highways, with interchanges, tunnels and signs
- The streets and lots of the town of Linden (no buildings yet)
- A clean starting area, with radiation rising the farther out you go. See [Radiation](../guide/radiation)

## Vanilla content

<StatusTag s="test" /> The vanilla content below can no longer be obtained in AFL and is not listed in the creative inventory

- Nether and End blocks, prismarine, lapis lazuli, redstone and sculk blocks, shulker boxes and beacons
- Vanilla functional blocks such as furnaces, blast furnaces, smokers, campfires, lanterns, candles, banners, flower pots, cauldrons and stonecutters
- Rails and minecarts
- Bows, crossbows and arrows
- Vanilla trees and wooden items; see [Forests](./forests#vanilla-wood)
- All vanilla tools (every axe, pickaxe, shovel and hoe included), buckets and bucketed liquids, fishing rods, compasses, clocks, maps and music discs
- All vanilla food and potions
- TNT, iron doors, iron trapdoors, lightning rods, chains, tinted glass, amethyst, totems of undying, tridents, saddles, fireworks, bone meal, ender pearls and goat horns
- Bricks, sandstone, red sandstone, mud bricks, reinforced deepslate, deepslate building blocks (cobbled, polished, bricks, tiles), copper blocks and cut copper, diamonds and emeralds
- Blaze rods, blaze powder, magma cream, ghast tears, phantom membranes, rabbit's feet, hearts of the sea, nautilus shells, scutes, glow ink sacs and slimeballs
- Pottery sherds and armor trim smithing templates
- Wither roses, torchflowers, pitcher plants, spore blossoms, glow lichen, dripleaf, pink petals, sea pickles, coral, sponges, mushroom blocks and mycelium; sniffer eggs, frogspawn and turtle eggs
- Blocks of iron, gold, coal, raw iron and raw gold, glazed terracotta, blue ice, slime blocks and honey blocks
- Iron bars (use AFL steel railings), enchanted books and bottles o' enchanting

Changes in the world:

- Lapis lazuli, redstone, diamond and emerald ore and amethyst geodes no longer generate
- Glow lichen, spore blossoms, dripleaf, coral, sea pickles, huge mushrooms, pink petals, fossils and blue ice no longer generate. Small mushrooms still do
- Natural sandstone and deepslate still generate but drop nothing when mined
- For now, new worlds give no way to mine stone and ore, fell trees or find food; AFL tools and food come later
- Apart from zombies (the infected), no vanilla mobs appear any more: no animals, villagers or other monsters. The creative inventory has no spawn eggs
- Vanilla blocks already placed still work but drop nothing when mined. Items and mobs already in old worlds are not deleted
- Vanilla items that stay: vanilla glass and glass panes (to be replaced by AFL glass panes later), glass bottles, eggs and snowballs
- Creative inventory: AFL tabs come first, vanilla tabs with nothing in them are hidden, and a page holds up to 15 tabs (5 each along the top, the left side and the bottom)
