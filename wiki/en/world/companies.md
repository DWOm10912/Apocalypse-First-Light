# Pre-war Companies

The Federation's pre-war companies come in all sizes and deal with each other the ordinary way. No conspiracies, defence contracts or big political events; real-world trouble is fine, such as a failing business, a product recall or a labour dispute

- Brand lettering, shop signs and nameplates in the game world are always English
- Chinese UI and readable documents (newspapers, notes, manuals, radio subtitles) use the Chinese company names
- Product names and model codes are never translated: Tamarack T15, P9-01, Silverwood 12

**Status:** frozen = names checked and approved by the user, renaming needs their approval; confirmed = set by the user or already in the game; candidate = design suggestion, not decided

<StatusTag s="planned" /> Brand visuals (wordmarks, colours, nameplate and sign specs) are not made yet; PRAIRIE, Halstead and Stanton come first

### PRAIRIE

- The company's legal name is left open; players only ever see PRAIRIE
- The price sign has two lines, GASOLINE / DIESEL
- Candidate: based in Sutton (S2); started as a fuel wholesaler delivering diesel to farms, later opened roadside stations; does not refine; the ordinary fuel stop every town has, lit all night

### Halstead

- Brand lettering HALSTEAD. Models are named after Federation trees (Tamarack is larch)
- The T15 is a single-cab diesel part-time 4WD pickup with a boxy 1990s look
- Candidate: one of the three big carmakers, head office and assembly plant in Kessler (S4); grew from a carriage body shop, known for tough farm trucks

### Kincaid

- Brand lettering KINCAID
- Candidate: based in Kessler, once a marine engine maker; supplies diesels to Halstead. The farmhouse's portable diesel generator could be its entry model and the A1 standby genset its industrial model. A brand would only change textures, never gameplay

### Laurel Valley Electric

- Candidate: office in Laurel Ridge (S1), serving Linden and the valleys around it; founded by farmers who paid for the lines themselves, buys its power from big plants. The farmhouse having no grid power could be explained by the co-op's lines not reaching that far (only a candidate explanation)

### Marais Cola

- Candidate: a regional company in Port Marais, grown from a harbour ice works and soda shop; an old southern brand also sold up north

### Dunleavy's Pharmacy

- Candidate: on Main St in Linden, run by one family for generations, with an old soda fountain; sells prescriptions, over-the-counter medicine and sundries, develops photos

### Stanton

- Brand lettering STANTON
- Candidate: an old nationwide pistol maker in an old riverside works in Bellhaven (S1); founded by a gunsmith family, started with revolvers; police and civilian self-defence, reliable, nothing fancy
- Why the P9 is "standard issue" and how it got into police stations stays unwritten for now: no procurement history
