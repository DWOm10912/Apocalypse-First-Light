# Setting

This page only holds confirmed setting. Anything marked "candidate" is a design suggestion: fine to draw on when writing, but not canon

## The Columbian Federation

Before the war, the Columbian Federation was one of the strongest industrial and military powers in the world. Within months of the nuclear strikes, the federal government fell apart

- Temperate, mostly plains, with big cities and a dense highway network
- North American in style, but its brands, companies and place names are the Federation's own; see [Pre-war Companies](./companies)
- The timeline is not locked: the setting never names a calendar year, history is told as "the early years of the nation" or "for generations"
- The capital is not decided yet

## Starting out

In the original setting the player starts in a shelter in the Federation's heartland. The V1 test build starts in the creek valley farmhouse by default and hides the bunker start for now

When creating a world you pick a start scene: creek valley farmhouse (default, up to 3 players), wilderness or roadside

- <StatusTag s="integrated" /> The farmhouse start is tied to its place: you begin at the foot of the porch steps
- <StatusTag s="planned" /> Wilderness and roadside are not tied to a place yet and start at the world spawn

## The six states

The Federal Six States are an island of about 20×16 km. State lines follow survey lines and one river. They only decide names, signs and building style, never terrain or biomes; the terrain column is roughly what each state looks like, and its name follows from it

| State | Name | Abbr. | Terrain | Style | Street names |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | New Kent | NK | Forested hills | Old English-style state | High St, Church St, Mill St |
| S2 | Arvonia | AV | Farm plains | Midwest | Courthouse Square, Depot St, Prairie Ave |
| S3 | Serrana | SR | Cold mountains | Mountain mining towns | Silver St, Ore Ave, Mine Rd |
| S4 | Laurentia | LT | Lake country | Great Lakes industry | Foundry St, Dock St, Lake Shore Dr |
| S5 | Marais | MR | Wetland coast | French bayou coast | Royal St, Toulouse St, Levee Rd |
| S6 | Altamesa | AT | Dry plateau | Spanish Southwest | Camino Real, Calle del Sol, Arroyo Rd |

- <StatusTag s="planned" /> The state names were set on 2026-10-22; nothing in the game shows them yet. Licence plates, road signs, maps and state line signs will carry the English names, plates with the full name across the top, maps, addresses and road signs with the two-letter abbreviation
- Port Marais is in Marais, written in full as Port Marais, Marais; Marais Cola takes the same name. Mesa Roja is in Altamesa

## Towns

The 16 towns have fixed names and sites (set by the user 2026-10-17): every world has the same map and the same names, and players explore each place themselves. Place names on signs are English in every language

- <StatusTag s="verified" d="2026-10-18" /> Only Linden has streets and lots; see [Places](./places#towns)

## Roads

The interstates are F-40 (east–west) and F-65 (north–south); state highways carry nationwide RT numbers, and road signs are English in every language. See [Roads](./roads)
